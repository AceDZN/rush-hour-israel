# שעת שיא — road rescue

The human selected **grab and rotate roads; rescue the bus**. The core is physical manipulation: lift a solid road, turn it, align its ports and snap it into a compact living diorama. The waiting bus responds immediately. See the current [design](docs/action-puzzle/DESIGN.md) and [art direction](docs/action-puzzle/ART_DIRECTION.md).

The first board has six children, a home pickup, a school and one missing straight. There is no setup phase, drawn service line, introductory failure clock or tool palette. The second adds a quarter turn. Drag and tap/keyboard equivalents perform the same operations; quick undo rewinds both the action and its modeled moment. A road in hand leaves a real gap. An occupied road cannot lift under its bus.

Twenty-four authored boards introduce finite pieces, distinct services, quiet land, narrow coast, matching ramp heights, supported water crossings, a one-way return-loop rescue, real bus–rail transfer, peripheral access, local clinic, conditional demand, closure and alternate plans. All required people must actually arrive. Optional move stars reward replay without blocking untimed completion.

The latest human feedback accepts this interaction and asks for more challenge and stronger graphics. Two short introductions now lead to four-to-eight-piece authored plans, legal route alternatives and globally incomplete allocations. The sample challenge is directly accessible. Untimed completion and quick recovery remain; extra waiting is not used to manufacture difficulty.

The current visual thesis is a restrained architectural model: natural limestone/concrete, fine asphalt, thin curbs, recessed Mediterranean facades and proportional school grounds under soft daylight. New original Tripo bus/school models, rigid figures and local audio explain the purpose. Fresh architectural targets preceded this art pass. See `docs/polish/`; previous toy-board images and easier-layout results remain historical. Human evaluation still determines challenge and visual acceptance.
