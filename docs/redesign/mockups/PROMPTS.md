## Immediate inspection and implementation decisions

Both tool outputs were displayed and visually inspected immediately. They show a substantial change in composition: inhabited places at useful screen scale, a recognizable school and bus, readable people, and a single spatial route gesture. The second image is the stronger attainable art reference because the trees/buildings are visibly simplified, although it still contains more small decorative detail than the first build should attempt.

Implement or prototype:

- The homes → waiting people → bus → school hierarchy, close camera, blue/green/terracotta palette and one obvious route gesture.
- Automatic usable bus stops, with route preview integrated into ordinary streets and attached to real places rather than anonymous node pins.
- The later image's two visible crossings, physical bridge queue, clear alternate route and small local queue feedback. River and low slope make the choice spatially meaningful.
- Actual 3D people can be simple original capsule/low-poly procedural forms initially. If deforming character models are introduced, the earlier Tripo rig requirement applies; these images do not supply a rigged asset.
- Existing original Tripo cream/navy/ochre bus identity, modeled school/courtyard, distinguishable homes and clinic, broadleaf vegetation clusters and coherent sidewalks.
- Minimal HUD with one current instruction and arrivals, small undo/pause. No budgets/timers/advanced tools in the introductory scene.

Reject or simplify:

- Image 01 drifted toward photographic detail despite the prompt. Use its framing and action, not its realistic masonry, individual roof tiles, foliage, detailed children or reflective water as a fidelity promise.
- Image 02 is still too prop-dense for immediate comprehension. Reduce benches, lamps, fences, parked cars and background homes; retain the two inhabited origins and two crossing choices. The river should read as a river into the sea, not an ambiguous decorative canal.
- The generated HUD is slightly oversized and some Hebrew is imperfect. Runtime must render real Hebrew text and clear passenger counts. Do not copy generated lettering into textures.
- First-route image does not demonstrate a wrong slope attempt or a complete arrival reward. Later repair image does not prove draining queues; those require actual moving model behavior and real play captures.
- No unrequested road-building accuracy or present-day infrastructure claim: original schematic teaching town, with coast west and terrain constraints controlled by the game model.
